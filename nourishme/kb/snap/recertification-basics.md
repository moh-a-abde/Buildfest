## What Is Recertification?

SNAP benefits are approved for a set period called a certification period. According to USDA FNS, when your certification period is about to end, you must go through recertification to continue receiving benefits. This is not a new application — it is a review to confirm that your household still qualifies and to update your benefit amount based on current circumstances.

## How Often Does Recertification Happen?

Certification periods vary. Most households are certified for 6 to 12 months. Certain groups, like elderly or disabled households with stable income, may receive longer certification periods of up to 24 months. Your state SNAP office sets the length of your certification period when you are approved. You will receive a recertification notice in the mail before your benefits expire, usually 30 to 60 days in advance.

## What You Will Need

During recertification you will typically need to provide updated information and documents, including:

- **Proof of identity** — driver's license, state ID, or other accepted identification
- **Proof of income** — recent pay stubs, benefit award letters, or a statement from an employer
- **Proof of expenses** — rent or mortgage receipts, utility bills, medical expenses (for elderly/disabled deductions), and child care costs
- **Household information** — names, dates of birth, and Social Security numbers for all household members

Your state may require an interview (phone or in-person) as part of the recertification process.

## Tips to Avoid a Gap in Benefits

Return your recertification paperwork as soon as you receive it — do not wait until the last day. Gather documents early, and if you are missing something, contact your caseworker to ask for extra time or alternative documentation. If your benefits lapse because you missed the deadline, you may need to reapply, which can take 30 days or more to process.

*For official eligibility information and state-specific recertification rules, contact your state SNAP office or visit [benefits.gov](https://benefits.gov).*
