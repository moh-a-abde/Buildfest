## SNAP-Eligible Foods

According to USDA Food and Nutrition Service (FNS), SNAP benefits can be used to buy food for the household to eat, including:

- **Fruits and vegetables** — fresh, frozen, canned, and dried
- **Meat, poultry, and fish**
- **Dairy products** — milk, cheese, yogurt
- **Breads and cereals**
- **Snack foods and non-alcoholic beverages**
- **Seeds and plants** that produce food for the household to eat

SNAP benefits can also be used to buy baby food and infant formula.

## What SNAP Cannot Buy

SNAP benefits **cannot** be used for alcoholic beverages, tobacco products, vitamins or supplements, medicines, hot prepared foods (food that is hot at the point of sale), or non-food items like pet food, cleaning supplies, paper products, or cosmetics. Live animals (except shellfish, fish removed from water, and animals slaughtered before purchase) are also not eligible.

## Where to Use SNAP

SNAP benefits are loaded onto an Electronic Benefits Transfer (EBT) card and can be used at authorized retail stores, many farmers markets, and some online grocery retailers (including Amazon, Walmart, and participating stores in USDA's online purchasing pilot). You can find authorized SNAP retailers near you using the USDA SNAP Retailer Locator.

*For official eligibility information and state-specific rules, contact your state SNAP office or visit [benefits.gov](https://benefits.gov).*
