## What Is an EBT Card?

An Electronic Benefits Transfer (EBT) card is a debit-like card issued by your state agency to deliver SNAP benefits. According to USDA FNS, all states use EBT cards to distribute SNAP benefits. Your monthly benefit amount is loaded onto the card automatically on a set date each month (the exact date varies by state and sometimes by the last digit of your case number).

## How to Use Your EBT Card

You use your EBT card at the checkout just like a debit card. Swipe or insert the card and enter your four-digit PIN. The purchase amount for eligible food items is deducted from your SNAP balance. You can check your remaining balance by looking at your receipt, calling the number on the back of your card, or using your state's EBT portal or mobile app.

## Protecting Your EBT Card

Keep your PIN private — never share it with anyone, including store employees. If your card is lost or stolen, contact your state EBT customer service line immediately to freeze the card and request a replacement. Benefits remaining on a lost or stolen card can typically be protected once you report the loss. According to FNS, each state sets its own timeline for issuing replacement cards, but most can do so within a few business days.

*For official eligibility information and state-specific rules, contact your state SNAP office or visit [benefits.gov](https://benefits.gov).*
